1 · Your chord progression

Supports major and minor triads, including sharps and flats. Each chord lasts one 4/4 bar.

2 · Compose your groove

Choose a bar, then tap squares to turn notes on or off. Each row is a chord tone. Only one note can play at each step; tapping a different row replaces it. A blank column is a rest.

16 steps per bar · Each numbered beat starts every four steps · R = root, 8 = octave, 5 = fifth, 3 = third
